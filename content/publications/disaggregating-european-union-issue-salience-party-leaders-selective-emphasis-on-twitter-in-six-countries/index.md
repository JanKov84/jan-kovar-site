  "abstract": "This article examines the internal composition of the salience dimension of European Union politicisation and asks how attention across its sub-issues relates to party-political characteristics, politicising moments and policy-field characteristics. Using 181,615 tweets by party leaders in six countries, it identifies 14,528 tweets related to the European Union, maps them across 21 issue categories, and analyses covariates of thematic emphasis. The results show concentrated and differentiated salience: governance dominates, macroeconomics, international affairs, and immigration form a second tier, and many categories remain episodic or marginal. Variation is systematically associated with party characteristics, crisis-related moments, and policy-field characteristics. Aggregate European Union salience therefore conceals substantial heterogeneity in the policy and polity objects that parties make visible across issues and over time.",
