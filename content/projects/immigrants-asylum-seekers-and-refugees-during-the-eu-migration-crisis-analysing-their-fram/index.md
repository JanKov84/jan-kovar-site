**2020–2022 · Selected past project**

**Funder:** Czech Science Foundation (GAČR)  
**Role:** Principal Investigator

This project examined how migration, migrants, asylum seekers, and refugees were portrayed in political and media discourse in Central and Eastern Europe. It investigated variation in framing across countries, political actors, media, migrant groups, and different phases of the European migration crisis.
