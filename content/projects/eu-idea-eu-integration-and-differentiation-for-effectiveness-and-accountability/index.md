**2019–2021 · Selected past project**

**Funder:** Horizon 2020  
**Role:** Research team member

EU IDEA examined differentiated integration as a feature of contemporary European governance and assessed its implications for effectiveness, accountability, and democratic legitimacy. My work focused particularly on differentiation from the perspective of smaller member states and on informal differentiated integration in EU foreign and security policy.
