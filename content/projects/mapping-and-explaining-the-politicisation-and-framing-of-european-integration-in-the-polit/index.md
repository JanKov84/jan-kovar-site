**2023–2025 · Selected past project**

**Funder:** Czech Science Foundation (GAČR)  
**Role:** Principal Investigator

This project examined how European integration was politicised and framed in political discourse across Central and Eastern Europe. It mapped variation in the salience and framing of European issues and investigated how political, institutional, and party-level factors help explain differences across actors, countries, and political contexts.
