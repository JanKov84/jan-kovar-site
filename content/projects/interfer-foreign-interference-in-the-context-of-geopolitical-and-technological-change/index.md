**2025–2028 · Current project**

**Funder:** OP JAK  
**Role:** Research team member

INTERFER examines foreign interference in the context of contemporary geopolitical and technological change. The project studies how foreign interference affects public attitudes, how states adapt to such interference domestically and through foreign policy, and how technological and geo-economic developments transform the instruments through which external actors seek to exert influence.
